BLOB BRAWL

Pick 3 wild abilities. Squish your friends. Last slime standing wins!

Controls & how to play
MoveA / D always move left / right on screen, even upside down. W / S climb walls. You stick to any side of an island.
JumpSPACE (jumps away from the surface you're stuck to)
AimMouse
AbilitiesLeft click = left ability · Middle click (scroll wheel) = middle · Right click = right
2nd playerArrow keys (↑ jump) · , / . / / for abilities
GamepadStick move & aim · A jump · X / Y / B abilities
RulesOne hit = splat. Water = splash. Bigger slimes eat smaller ones. Getting squished between islands is deadly. Last slime standing wins the round. After 45s, sudden death: the sea rises and the islands drift together.
