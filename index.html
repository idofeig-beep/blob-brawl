BLOB BRAWL

Pick 3 wild abilities. Squish your friends. Last slime standing wins!

Controls & how to play
Mouse + keysA/D move · W or Space jump · aim with mouse · Left click / Right click / E = abilities 1/2/3
WASDA/D move · W jump · W/S aim up/down · F / G / H = abilities
Arrows←/→ move · ↑ jump · ↑/↓ aim · , / . / / = abilities
GamepadStick move & aim (right stick aims too) · A jump · X / Y / B = abilities
RulesOne hit = splat. Water = splash. Last one standing wins the round. First to the target score wins. After each round, everyone except the winner may swap one ability.
